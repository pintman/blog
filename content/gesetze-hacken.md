Was passiert, wenn man ein LLM auf Schwachstellen in Gesetzen und Regeln ansetzt? Das hier:

> In testing this hypothesis, we have identified a substantial number of vulnerabilities in the German legal system using LLMs. To our knowledge, none of these have been previously recorded. We share a selection of vulnerabilities below, and present potential exploits for each. We share only those vulnerabilities whose exploitation we believe would not cause serious harm.

Quelle: [Legal exploits may emerge at scale](https://www.felixringe.com/legal-exploits/) von Felix Ringe und Linus Folkerts (28.10.2026)

Inklusive eines Beispiels (samt Exploit), wie Journalist\*innen über das IFG an vertrauliche Stellungnahmen eines Beratungsgremiums gelangen könnten.

> In brief: A rule that makes certain documents confidential may be void because the government failed to cite the legal basis it needed to make that rule.
